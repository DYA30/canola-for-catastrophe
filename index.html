The problem

A sudden global cooling would leave the world short of protein.

Photo · canola meal or field · 16:9

  • The shock

    A nuclear winter, a large volcanic eruption or an asteroid impact could block sunlight and cool the planet for years. Conventional crop yields would fall sharply, and affordable, nutritious protein would get scarce.

  • The overlooked resource

    Canola meal is what's left after the oil is pressed out of the seed. It's rich in protein but mostly fed to livestock. With the right processing, that protein can be extracted for people to eat.

  • The gap

    Extraction methods already exist. What nobody knows yet is how quickly they could be scaled up when it matters most, and which one is actually worth scaling.

What we're doing about it

  1. CompareReview existing protein-extraction methods side by side.
  2. SelectScore them on yield, cost and scalability, and pick the most viable.
  3. ModelEstimate how fast the chosen process could scale in a catastrophe.

Where it ends up: findings are written up as a peer-reviewed journal article with ALLFED.
